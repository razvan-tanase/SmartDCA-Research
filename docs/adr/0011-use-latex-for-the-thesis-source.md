# Use LaTeX as the authoritative thesis source and derive rendered releases

The thesis needs one source that can carry equations, cross-references,
bibliography entries, appendices, and generated evidence assets without manual
transcription. Rendered submission files must remain reproducible outputs, not
independent editable copies.

The available official material was inspected before selecting the
source/build seam. The [retained Senate-approved 2025-2026 finalization
regulation](../../references/institutional/politehnica-finalization-regulation-2025-2026.pdf)
and its Annex 1b/2 forms establish current university-level requirements. The
candidate-supplied [official faculty thesis
template](../../references/institutional/acs-official-thesis-template-2018.pdf)
was inspected in full on 2026-09-02. It was itself generated by LaTeX,
explicitly supports LaTeX citations and a separate BibTeX file, and prescribes
the bilingual cover/front-matter route while recommending A4, 25.4 mm margins,
12 pt justified text, 1.5 line spacing, and a 14/14/12 pt heading hierarchy.
The [institutional
contract](../../manuscript/contract/institutional-contract.md#verified-faculty-template-requirements)
records its exact digest, bounded authority, and the fields it leaves open.

Keep LaTeX under `manuscript/source/` as the authoritative manuscript source,
BibTeX under `manuscript/bibliography/` as the bibliography authority, and
generated TeX fragments under `manuscript/generated/` as the generated-asset
seam. Use `manuscript/build.py` to derive the PDF. Never edit a rendered PDF as
manuscript source.

Implement the retained template as the source/style layer and verify the
rendered document before continuous drafting. Current university identity and
the 2025-2026 originality declaration take precedence over the template's 2018
cover wording. If a newer official template requires an authoritative format
that cannot be represented faithfully from this source, revisit this decision
before prose expands rather than maintaining two authoritative manuscripts.
