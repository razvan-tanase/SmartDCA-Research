publication within its stated scope. An independent standard-library replay
regenerated the accepted deterministic study and cross-layer synthesis in new
output roots, reproduced a registered historical slice from the retained
Yahoo Finance inputs, and reconciled the complete primary historical,
block-bootstrap, Holm, and raw registered-robustness evidence. The review found

each report now declares its final role, links this review, scopes earlier
no-execution statements to its own run, and uses the registered CPython 3.12
route. No accepted protocol, input, source receipt, or run bundle was edited.

## Accounting and statistical audit

The independent recurrence calculates DCA through its own full-deposit route
and the two guarded policies through one floor recurrence, differing only in
the discretionary score. Across the deterministic replay and registered
historical slice it rechecked nonnegative cash and purchases, nondecreasing
units, full funding including fees, direct cash-plus-units terminal wealth,
frictionless unit coverage, shared floor semantics, terminal cash/unit
attribution, and decision stability under every truncated prefix. The
`lambda=1` transaction-level collapse and finite frictionless floor checks also
reconciled across all 594 and 612 applicable synthesis cells, respectively.
These finite checks audit the implementation; the universal frictionless
guarantee still comes only from the epsilon-DCA theorem.[^guardrail]

The raw historical regrouping reproduced 18 negative H1 medians, 17 negative
and one positive H2 median, and 18 negative secondary architecture medians.
All 36 independently regenerated bootstrap statistic streams matched their
saved SHA-256 values, percentile intervals, centered tail counts, finite-run
p-values, and seeds. Full-family Holm recomputation retained nine H1
rejections and no H2 rejection. An independent regrouping of all 810
robustness aggregate cells also matched the saved cells: all 30 monthly and 48
quarterly non-unit frictionless complete-system medians were negative, while
the quarterly signal slice retained 40 negative and eight positive medians.
Robustness rows remain descriptive and outside the sealed 36-test family.

Gross and net scopes remain disjoint. Only frictionless rows carry the current
epsilon-DCA theorem scope. Every proportional- and fixed-fee row is labeled
`outside-current-safety-theorem`; its performance is empirical net-of-cost
evidence, not a theorem audit. No cost-adjusted shortfall is described as a
failure of the frictionless safety result.

## Claim and writing audit

Every thesis-facing numerical statement was traced to raw episode results or
the versioned synthesis evidence. Complete-system, corrected-mean signal, and
safety-architecture comparisons remain separate. Confirmatory H1/H2
statements use only the preregistered primary cells and one 36-test Holm
family; architecture, robustness, cost, deterministic, stochastic, and sparse
BTC quarterly statements keep their descriptive or exploratory labels.

The strongest supported conclusion is negative and bounded: the chosen
corrected-mean signal did not establish incremental historical value, and the
complete system had a negative median relative-wealth gap in every non-unit
primary historical cell. The evidence does not establish equivalence, a zero
signal, causal market effects, expected performance, parameter optimality, or
universal superiority or inferiority. Simulation and historical observations
remain empirical evidence, never proof of a universal policy ordering.

The six publication-ready reports now agree on those boundaries:

- the canonical run is infrastructure evidence;
- deterministic paths are finite mechanism and stress evidence;
- stochastic families are controlled sensitivity, not calibrated market
  probabilities;
- the historical seam is input/provenance infrastructure rather than a policy
  result;
- the confirmatory report carries the registered historical inference and the
  separately labeled robustness extension; and
- the synthesis is the final thesis-facing empirical checkpoint.

## Findings and resolution

No scientific or statistical blocker was found. The actionable review findings
were stale pre-publication state in six reports, generic interpreter commands
in three reports, and the need to preserve the final independent replay and
failure route as a named repository check. All were resolved. A forced partial
private invocation now records a typed failure before aborting; the successful
route retains an empty failure ledger rather than silently omitting failure
state.

No canonical vocabulary or model assumption changed, so `CONTEXT.md` remains
unchanged. No surprising hard-to-reverse trade-off was introduced, so no new
ADR is warranted. Accepted scientific source bytes, protocols, inputs, and run
bundles remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

## Publication conclusion

The empirical effort is complete. The package contains immutable inputs and
receipts, reviewed synthetic and historical runs, raw private evidence under
the access-controlled private-retention boundary, public aggregates and
uncertainty, generated
tables and figure-ready data, executable accounting and statistical checks,
and one conservative synthesis. It supports a proved frictionless safety floor
and a measured safety-adaptivity trade-off, but no universal, causal, optimal,
or confirmed incremental superiority claim for the corrected-mean signal.

The next project decision is manuscript assembly. New data, a refreshed
historical cutoff, alternate corrected-mean parameters, a different signal,
or a changed guardrail requires a separately identified protocol, artifact,
or effort rather than mutation of this package.
