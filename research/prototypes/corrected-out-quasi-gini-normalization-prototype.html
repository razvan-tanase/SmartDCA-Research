Which correction should become canonical?

This demo compares the smallest common-weight repairs of the source out quasi-Gini functional. The question is whether the candidates are internal means with both compatibility identities and a finite diagonal, and which candidate best preserves the source's SmartDCA parameter semantics.

Boundary carried forward from ticket 04: choosing a mean does not prove economic dominance over DCA. Any practical allocation still needs an explicit causal budget map and a restricted path class or performance criterion.
