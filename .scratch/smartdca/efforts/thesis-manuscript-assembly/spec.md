## Question

How can the completed mathematical and empirical SmartDCA research be assembled
into a coherent, submission-ready Financial Computing master's thesis whose
claims are traceable to reviewed evidence, whose negative empirical result is
reported honestly, and whose main narrative remains understandable without
making detailed proofs the center of the defense?

## Problem Statement

The research program has reached manuscript readiness. It now contains the
source-functional audit, the corrected out quasi-Gini mean, the causal DCA
impossibility boundary, the sharp epsilon-DCA unit guardrail, a guarded
corrected-mean SmartDCA rule, finite- and arbitrary-horizon performance
boundaries, a preregistered empirical protocol, deterministic and stochastic
studies, confirmatory historical evaluation, registered robustness evidence,
and an independent publication review.

Closed research tickets do not by themselves form a thesis. The repository
does not yet provide one institutional manuscript contract, one chapter
architecture, one bibliography, one contribution and non-claim matrix, or one
claim-to-evidence map connecting every theorem, empirical statement, table, and
figure to its authority. Drafting chapters independently without those controls
would invite duplicated explanations, inconsistent notation, citation drift,
manual transcription errors, and overstatement of either the corrected-mean
construction or its empirical performance.

The thesis also has a communication constraint. It belongs to a Financial
Computing master's program, so the body and defense narrative should emphasize
the financial problem, causal algorithm, exact accounting, safety design, and
reproducible evaluation. Mathematical proofs remain necessary, but detailed
machinery should support the story from appendices rather than displace it.

The empirical result must be treated as a scientific result, not optimized
away. The frozen evaluation supports the model-free frictionless safety floor
but found no confirmed incremental historical value from the corrected-mean
signal. The complete system had a negative median gap against DCA in every
non-unit primary frictionless historical cell. This makes the distinction
between guaranteed safety and demonstrated adaptivity the thesis's central
finding, not a reason to reopen parameter selection.

## Solution

Build one evidence-first thesis manuscript and accept it through one complete
release seam.

Use the provisional title:

> Safe Adaptivity in Dollar-Cost Averaging: From an Impossibility Boundary to a
> Reproducible Empirical Evaluation

Organize the thesis around three research questions:

1. Can a sequentially admissible, fully funded strategy universally dominate
   the same-deposit DCA comparator?
2. Can an economically distinct adaptive strategy retain a transparent,
   model-free relative-wealth floor?
3. Does the guarded corrected-mean score add empirical value inside that safety
   architecture?

The corresponding answers are:

1. Universal pathwise dominance forces the strategy to purchase exactly as DCA.
2. The epsilon-DCA unit guardrail gives the sharp causal frictionless safety
   floor and leaves a funded discretionary allocation.
3. The frozen empirical evaluation confirms the safety architecture but does
   not confirm incremental value from the corrected-mean signal.

Assemble the work in an evidence-first sequence:

1. Freeze the institutional manuscript contract: official template, language,
   submission date, expected extent, required front matter, citation style,
   formatting rules, archival requirements, and supervisor-specific
   constraints.
2. Create the contribution and non-claim matrix, chapter skeleton, notation
   register, and claim-to-evidence map before drafting continuous prose.
3. Consolidate the literature review and bibliography around DCA and adaptive
   accumulation, generalized and Bajraktarević means, sequential portfolio
   decisions and safety constraints, and reproducible computational finance.
4. Draft the financial model, source audit, corrected construction,
   impossibility boundary, safety architecture, policy implementation, and
   analytical performance-boundary material.
5. Draft the preregistered empirical methodology and the deterministic,
   stochastic, confirmatory historical, and registered robustness results
   without pooling their inferential units.
6. Draft the introduction, related work synthesis, discussion, limitations,
   conclusion, and abstract after the technical and empirical chapters are
   stable.
7. Build the complete release, reproduce every included table and figure from
   authoritative artifacts, perform independent claim and citation audits,
   integrate supervisor feedback, and produce the submission PDF.

The main text should carry definitions, assumptions, theorem meaning, policy
logic, decisive equations, empirical design, results, and interpretation.
Longer proofs, exhaustive cases, supplementary tables, detailed validation
records, and reproduction instructions belong in appendices or linked
supplementary material. The manuscript must remain self-contained enough for a
committee reader while preserving the repository as the authoritative evidence
layer.

The complete release seam is the acceptance surface. One canonical manuscript
source must produce the body, appendices, bibliography, tables, figures, and
submission PDF. A release passes only when every scientific and numerical claim
is represented in the claim-to-evidence map, every included computed artifact
is regenerated or reconciled against frozen evidence, institutional
requirements are satisfied, and an independent audit finds no claim, citation,
statistical-language, reproducibility, or presentation blocker.

## Outcome requirements

- Deliver a submission-ready master's thesis and reproducible complete release
  package conforming to the frozen institutional contract.
- Preserve the narrative sequence: adaptive-superiority ambition, causal
  impossibility boundary, attainable safety relaxation, guarded computational
  policy, exact performance boundaries, preregistered evaluation, and
  informative negative result.
- State clearly which contributions are mathematical, computational,
  methodological, empirical, or integrative and which novelty claims are not
  made.
- Keep the corrected construction positioned within the known weighted
  Bajraktarević family rather than presenting it as a newly discovered mean
  class.
- Keep epsilon-DCA safety separate from economic dominance and from observed
  outperformance.
- Present complete-system performance against DCA separately from signal-only
  performance against the neutral guarded selector.
- Preserve the primary confirmatory run and separately registered robustness
  run as distinct evidence, with their declared inferential limits.
- Generate or reconcile every manuscript table and figure from authoritative,
  versioned evidence rather than manual transcription.
- Make every theorem, numerical claim, citation-dependent claim, table, and
  figure traceable through the claim-to-evidence map.
- Place detailed proof machinery where it supports rather than obscures the
  Financial Computing narrative.
- Include limitations that distinguish the frictionless theorem from
  net-of-cost empirical results and realized evidence from universal claims.
- Complete independent domain, citation, statistical-language, reproducibility,
  and visual release review before submission.
- Treat any new data, parameter tuning, signal, guardrail, or outcome-driven
  analysis as a separately specified and, where appropriate, preregistered
  research effort.

## Implementation Decisions

- Treat the thesis as the primary deliverable. A journal or conference paper,
  venue selection, and defense deck are later derivations rather than parallel
  acceptance targets.
- Use the proposed title as a working title until the institutional manuscript
  contract and supervisor guidance are frozen.
- Freeze the official template, language, submission deadline, extent,
  front-matter requirements, citation style, archival rules, and
  supervisor-specific constraints before committing to document-specific
  formatting.
- Select the canonical authoring and build format only after inspecting the
  official template. Preserve a single authoritative manuscript source and
  treat rendered documents as derived releases.
- Use one complete manuscript release as the highest verification seam.
  Chapter reviews are intermediate feedback gates and do not replace final
  integration review.
- Preserve the three approved research questions and their conservative
  answers as the thesis spine. Chapter titles may change without changing this
  contract when the same argument remains intact.
- Use an evidence-first drafting order: manuscript contract, contribution and
  non-claim matrix, claim-to-evidence map, chapter skeleton, notation register,
  literature synthesis, technical chapters, empirical chapters, integrative
  chapters, abstract, then release audit.
- Use a contribution taxonomy that distinguishes mathematical results,
  computational artifacts, empirical methodology, empirical findings, and
  integrative thesis synthesis.
- Record explicit non-claims, including no new general mean class, no universal
  SmartDCA superiority, no confirmed incremental historical value from the
  corrected-mean signal, and no extension of the safety theorem to frictions.
- Structure the core narrative around the financial comparison model, the
  impossibility boundary, the epsilon-DCA safety relaxation, the guarded
  policy, the exact performance boundaries, and the empirical evaluation.
  Present the source-functional correction and prior mean theory at the depth
  needed to justify the chosen signal.
- Keep the main text readable to a Financial Computing committee. Include
  decisive definitions, equations, theorem statements, proof ideas, algorithm
  logic, and empirical interpretation in the body; place long proofs, exhaustive
  cases, and supplementary validation detail in appendices.
- Use the domain glossary as the terminology authority. In particular, preserve
  sequential admissibility, deposit budget, DCA comparator, economic dominance,
  epsilon-DCA safety, epsilon-DCA unit guardrail, funded discretionary
  allocation, terminal wealth, and average acquisition cost with their
  established claim boundaries.
- Preserve the comparison model of finite positive prices, exogenous deposits,
  causal long-only buy-only purchases, no leverage, cash carried without
  interest, and cash-inclusive terminal wealth.
- Present the corrected out quasi-Gini mean as a weighted Bajraktarević mean
  and restrict homogeneity and monotonicity statements to their proved
  parameter-transform regions.
- Attribute the relative-wealth floor to the unit guardrail and treat the
  corrected-mean score only as a selector within the funded discretionary
  allocation.
- Keep complete-system, signal-only, and architecture-only comparisons
  distinct. Use identical economic conditions when comparing DCA, the neutral
  guarded selector, and the corrected guarded rule.
- Preserve the confirmatory historical evaluation as primary and the separately
  registered robustness evaluation as post-confirmatory descriptive evidence.
  Do not use robustness outcomes to rewrite the registered hypotheses.
- Keep deterministic paths, seeded stochastic families, primary historical
  episodes, and registered robustness evidence in separate reporting layers.
  A cross-layer synthesis may compare patterns but may not pool incompatible
  inferential units.
- Generate manuscript tables and figure-ready graphics from the reviewed
  artifacts. Hand-edited copies are presentation outputs, not authoritative
  computations.
- Give every scientific claim, quantitative claim, table, and figure a stable
  entry in the claim-to-evidence matrix. Record its wording, evidence authority,
  claim class, scope, manuscript location, and review state.
- Give every externally dependent factual or novelty-positioning claim a
  bibliography entry and citation. Prefer primary mathematical literature,
  official methodological sources, and original study reports.
- Consolidate literature around four bounded areas: DCA and adaptive
  accumulation, generalized and Bajraktarević means, causal portfolio
  decisions and safety constraints, and reproducible computational finance.
- Maintain one notation register and introduce every symbol before use.
  Reconcile notation differences between theorem artifacts and the manuscript
  explicitly rather than silently renaming quantities.
- Preserve retained source and provider-data restrictions. The thesis may
  report reviewed aggregates and sanitized provenance but must not redistribute
  observations whose receipt declares them private.
- Include a reproducibility statement that identifies the accepted software,
  protocols, run identities, input receipts, environment assumptions, and
  independent review without requiring hidden conversation context.
- Track supervisor changes by concern and resolution. A requested scientific
  change that would alter frozen evidence is not an editorial revision and
  must follow the new-research rule.
- Freeze each submission candidate with a release identity binding manuscript
  source, bibliography, generated assets, evidence-map state, and rendered PDF.
- Treat new datasets, parameters, signals, guardrails, hypotheses, or
  outcome-driven analyses as a separate effort. Do not tune the thesis evidence
  in response to an unattractive result.

## Testing Decisions

- Test the complete release as external behavior: from the declared source
  state and environment, one documented build produces the expected body,
  appendices, bibliography, tables, figures, and submission PDF.
- Prefer end-to-end release checks over tests of manuscript helper internals.
  A good test observes a reader-, reviewer-, or submitter-visible property.
- Verify the institutional contract against the rendered release: required
  front matter, order, page geometry, typography, numbering, language, citation
  style, file format, metadata, and archival constraints.
- Fail the release when required institutional or supervisor inputs remain as
  placeholders or unresolved decisions.
- Verify that every scientific claim, quantitative claim, table, and figure in
  the manuscript has exactly one current claim-to-evidence entry and that every
  entry points to an existing authoritative artifact.
- Verify theorem statements, assumptions, definitions, and equality or
  inequality directions against their canonical theorem or definition pages.
- Verify numerical statements by regeneration from accepted outputs or by
  independent reconciliation against their immutable run bundles and review
  receipts.
- Verify manuscript tables and figures from generated source data, including
  labels, units, sample counts, exclusions, coverage values, uncertainty
  summaries, and primary-versus-robustness designation.
- Verify that the lambda-equals-one collapse, the frictionless safety floor,
  and transaction-cost scope are described consistently across methodology,
  results, discussion, and conclusion.
- Verify that complete-system results against DCA are never substituted for
  signal-only results against the neutral guarded selector.
- Verify that deterministic, stochastic, historical, and robustness evidence
  retain their declared inferential roles and are not pooled into an
  unsupported probability or significance claim.
- Verify registered hypotheses, multiplicity handling, block-resampling
  language, sample units, and statistical conclusions against the frozen
  empirical protocol and reviewed reports.
- Verify every external citation resolves, supports the nearby claim, is
  represented in the bibliography, and follows the required style.
- Independently audit novelty wording, especially the corrected mean's known
  Bajraktarević identification and the boundary between project results and
  prior theory.
- Verify terminology and notation against the glossary and notation register.
  Flag every use of dominance, safety, outperformance, causality, mean, and
  acquisition cost that crosses its accepted claim boundary.
- Verify internal references, theorem numbering, equation numbering, table and
  figure numbering, appendix references, glossary use, acronyms, and
  bibliography links from the rendered document.
- Render and visually inspect every page. Reject clipped content, unreadable
  plots, broken equations, widows or orphans that materially impair reading,
  missing glyphs, inconsistent captions, unresolved references, blank pages
  outside the template contract, and inaccessible color choices.
- Verify that figure captions and table notes are interpretable without relying
  on hidden repository context and state the relevant population, units,
  policy comparison, and evidence layer.
- Verify that the abstract, introduction, contribution summary, discussion, and
  conclusion make mutually consistent claims and do not strengthen detailed
  results.
- Run the repository-wide link and scientific verification suite before
  freezing a release, then run the manuscript-specific build and audit checks.
- Perform an independent domain and specification review after drafting and an
  independent release review after all supervisor feedback is integrated.
- Require the final review to reproduce every included generated asset or
  reconcile it to the accepted evidence, sample-check proof-to-theorem
  consistency, audit citations and statistics, and report whether any
  submission blocker remains.
- Use the existing exact scientific checks, immutable empirical run identities,
  claim-limited evidence notes, independent empirical-package review, and
  repository link checker as prior art for the manuscript release tests.

## Out of Scope

- Inventing, tuning, or selecting a new adaptive signal from thesis outcomes.
- Changing the corrected-mean parameters, transform, deposit rules, safety
  factor grid, guardrail, datasets, hypotheses, or registered inference to
  improve the reported result.
- Reopening the immutable confirmatory evaluation except for deterministic
  reproduction or reconciliation required by release review.
- Claiming universal, stochastic, causal, or optimal SmartDCA superiority.
- Proving epsilon-DCA safety under transaction costs, taxes, slippage, cash
  interest, dividends outside declared series semantics, or market impact.
- Claiming that the corrected out quasi-Gini construction is a new general mean
  class.
- Expanding the empirical universe beyond the frozen assets, schedules,
  horizons, and registered robustness inputs.
- Developing a production trading system, broker integration, live execution,
  custody workflow, or individualized investment advice.
- Writing and submitting a separate journal or conference paper.
- Selecting a publication venue.
- Producing the defense slide deck, speech, demonstration, or question bank.
- Translating the complete thesis into additional languages unless the
  institutional contract requires it.
- Treating supervisor or committee approval as automatable; the effort prepares
  the artifact and evidence needed for those human decisions.

## Further Notes

This effort converts a completed research package into a thesis; it does not
change the scientific outcome. The central contribution is the full
safety-adaptivity inquiry: a universal-superiority ambition is bounded by
impossibility, relaxed into an exact safety architecture, implemented as a
causal policy, and evaluated through a frozen reproducible protocol that does
not confirm value from the selected adaptive signal.

The [independent empirical-package review](../../../../research/notes/safety-adaptivity-empirical-package-review.md)
is the publication-readiness gate for the frozen evidence. The
[repository introduction](../../../../README.md) is the canonical result index,
and the [domain glossary](../../../../CONTEXT.md) governs manuscript
terminology. The approved verification seam is one complete manuscript release
with a claim-to-evidence matrix and independent audit.

The next workflow step after this specification is approved and published is
ticket decomposition. Implementation should begin with the manuscript contract,
skeleton, contribution and non-claim matrix, notation register, and evidence
map rather than Chapter 1 prose.
