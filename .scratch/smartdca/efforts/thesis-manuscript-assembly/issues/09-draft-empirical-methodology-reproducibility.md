## Question

Can a reviewer reconstruct the complete empirical design and its inferential limits before reading outcomes, including how each policy, comparison, evidence layer, and immutable artifact is defined?

## What to build

A reviewer can understand and reproduce the registered evaluation from declared protocols, inputs or receipts, policies, estimands, statistics, run identities, and environment assumptions without hidden conversation context.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The chapter distinguishes the DCA comparator, neutral guarded selector, and corrected guarded rule under identical timing, deposits, horizons, and costs.
- [ ] Complete-system, signal-only, and architecture-only comparisons are defined separately.
- [ ] Historical series semantics, provider provenance, source receipts, retained-input fingerprints, private-data restrictions, and redistribution boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Deterministic paths, seeded stochastic families, primary rolling historical episodes, and registered robustness evidence have separate declared roles.
- [ ] Deposit schedules, horizons, evaluation dates, safety factors, corrected-mean configuration, cost scenarios, exclusions, and validation rules match the frozen protocol.
- [ ] Estimands, downside summaries, mechanism measures, block resampling, finite-run p-values, multiplicity family, and Holm adjustment are stated exactly.
- [ ] Confirmatory hypotheses, secondary analyses, registered robustness, and exploratory interpretation remain distinct.
- [ ] Frictionless theorem coverage and net-of-cost empirical scope are separated before results are introduced.
- [ ] The reproducibility statement identifies accepted software, environment, protocols, input identities, run identities, review receipts, and clean build routes.
- [ ] Every methodological statement is mapped to protocol or literature authority, independently checked, and rendered successfully.
