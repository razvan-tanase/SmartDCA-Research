## Question

Which methodological literature supports the thesis's preregistration, immutable evidence, overlapping-window inference, multiplicity control, and reproducible computational-finance release design?

## What to build

A methods reviewer can trace each material empirical and reproducibility choice to an authoritative source and distinguish registered inference from descriptive robustness and engineering provenance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The review covers reproducible computational research, preregistration or outcome-blind design, immutable or provenance-bound artifacts, overlapping time-series episodes, block resampling, multiplicity control, and confirmatory-versus-exploratory reporting.
- [ ] Original statistical and official methodological sources are preferred over tertiary summaries.
- [ ] The literature explains what overlapping episodes change about uncertainty without claiming that dependence-aware methods create causal identification.
- [ ] Holm adjustment, resampling language, inferential units, effect-size reporting, and descriptive robustness are positioned accurately.
- [ ] Reproducibility claims distinguish deterministic regeneration, independent reconciliation, provider-data receipts, and public redistribution.
- [ ] The manuscript section does not overstate the authority of software engineering practices as financial evidence.
- [ ] Every methodological assertion has a bibliography entry and claim-to-evidence record.
- [ ] Independent citation and statistical-language review passes, and the canonical build remains clean.
