## Question

Can the supervisor's real feedback be integrated transparently while preserving the distinction between editorial revision, interpretive clarification, and newly authorized scientific work?

## What to build

The candidate and supervisor can inspect one concern-and-resolution log, see how every comment affected the manuscript, and receive a revised final candidate whose scientific evidence boundaries remain intact.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The independently audited candidate is presented through the agreed review package, and actual supervisor comments are retained with enough context to resolve them.
- [ ] Every concern is classified as institutional, structural, editorial, citation, mathematical, computational, empirical, presentation, or proposed new research.
- [ ] Each received concern records its disposition, manuscript impact, responsible party, and verification state.
- [ ] Editorial and presentational changes preserve theorem, protocol, run, and evidence identities.
- [ ] Any request for new data, tuning, signals, guardrails, hypotheses, or outcome-driven analysis is separated into a newly authorized effort rather than folded into the frozen thesis evidence.
- [ ] Changes to claims, citations, statistics, tables, figures, notation, or layout trigger the corresponding focused checks and evidence-map updates.
- [ ] The complete manuscript rebuilds after all accepted revisions, and affected pages receive visual review.
- [ ] No required supervisor concern remains unresolved or hidden before the ticket is resolved.
- [ ] The record does not claim to automate supervisor or committee approval.
- [ ] The revised candidate is ready for an independent post-feedback release review.
