## Question

Does the first complete release satisfy the specification, support every claim and citation, reproduce its quantitative content, and render professionally enough to present to the supervisor?

## What to build

An independent reviewer can reproduce the candidate, inspect its evidence and presentation, record typed findings, and leave either a supervisor-ready corrected release or explicit unresolved blockers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An independent specification review checks every outcome, implementation, testing, and exclusion decision in the approved contract.
- [ ] An independent domain review verifies definitions, assumptions, theorem wording, proof summaries, equality directions, terminology, and novelty boundaries.
- [ ] Every external citation is checked for resolution, nearby support, attribution accuracy, bibliography presence, and required style.
- [ ] Registered hypotheses, inferential units, resampling, multiplicity, estimates, uncertainty, and statistical conclusions are reconciled independently.
- [ ] Every included generated asset is reproduced or reconciled, and representative theorem-to-proof and number-to-run paths are sampled without relying only on producer assertions.
- [ ] The abstract, introduction, contribution summary, results, discussion, limitations, and conclusion are checked for headline claim drift.
- [ ] Every rendered page is visually inspected for equations, glyphs, clipping, typography, captions, tables, plots, page breaks, accessibility, and template compliance.
- [ ] The complete repository scientific suite, manuscript checks, clean build, and link audit pass.
- [ ] All actionable findings are resolved and rechecked; anything unresolved is recorded as a supervisor-review blocker rather than waived silently.
- [ ] The corrected candidate and audit record are understandable without hidden conversation context.
